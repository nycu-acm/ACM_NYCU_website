Keynote Speaker at MAPR 2026!

We are delighted to share that our professor was invited as a keynote speaker at MAPR 2026, held on August 13–14, 2026, at HCM-UTE in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

This is a wonderful opportunity to share research insights, exchange innovative ideas, and strengthen international academic collaboration.

🎉 主題演講｜MAPR 2026！

很高興與大家分享，我們的教授受邀擔任 MAPR 2026 國際研討會的主題演講嘉賓。本次會議於 2026 年 8 月 13 日至 14 日在越南胡志明市的 HCM-UTE 舉行。

這是分享研究見解、交流創新思維及促進國際學術合作的寶貴機會！
