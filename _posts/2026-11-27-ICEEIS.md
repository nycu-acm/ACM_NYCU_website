We are delighted to share that our professor was invited as a keynote speaker at ICEEIS 2026, held on November 26–27, 2026, at HCM-UTE in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. This event provides a valuable opportunity to share research insights, exchange ideas, and strengthen international academic collaboration.

很高興與大家分享，我們的教授受邀擔任 ICEEIS 2026 國際研討會的主題演講嘉賓。此次會議於 2026 年 11 月 26 日至 27 日在越南胡志明市的 HCM-UTE 舉行，為分享研究成果、交流學術觀點及促進國際學術合作提供了寶貴的機會。
