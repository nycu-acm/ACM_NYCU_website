📸✨ We are proud to share that our research team has five papers at the **IEEE International Conference on Image Processing (ICIP) 2026**, showcasing our latest research in image processing, video editing, remote sensing, and 3D vision!

Our featured works include:

1. [ADAPT: Any-codec Diffusion-based Adaptation for Image Perception-Distortion Tradeoff](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11630121)
2. [IDAG-Edit: Multi-Object Video Editing via Instance-Decoupled Attention and Guidance](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22042v1)
3. [PaTSeg: A Patch-Level Text Supervision Paradigm for Mask-Free Remote Sensing Segmentation](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11630128)
4. [TEXT-PILOT: Intelligent Visual Text Planning and Manipulation via Multi-Modal LLM as Agent](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11630578)
5. [Multi-Light Relightable Gaussian Splatting with Phong Reflectance from in-the-wild images](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11629888)

Congratulations to all researchers for their outstanding contributions and achievements! 🎉👏
