Our lab at ICIP 2026

October 16, 2026 October 19, 2026
research-activity

📸✨ We are proud to share that our research team has five papers at the IEEE International Conference on Image Processing (ICIP) 2026, showcasing our latest research in image processing, video editing, remote sensing, and 3D vision!

Our featured works include:

  1. ADAPT: Any-codec Diffusion-based Adaptation for Image Perception-Distortion Tradeoff
  2. IDAG-Edit: Multi-Object Video Editing via Instance-Decoupled Attention and Guidance
  3. PaTSeg: A Patch-Level Text Supervision Paradigm for Mask-Free Remote Sensing Segmentation
  4. TEXT-PILOT: Intelligent Visual Text Planning and Manipulation via Multi-Modal LLM as Agent
  5. Multi-Light Relightable Gaussian Splatting with Phong Reflectance from in-the-wild images

Congratulations to all researchers for their outstanding contributions and achievements! 🎉👏
